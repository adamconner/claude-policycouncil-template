## Executive Summary

This Phase 1 ideation generated 30 policy proposals from 6 diverse perspectives on increasing affordable housing availability in major US cities. Approximately 40% of proposals are **[BIG IDEA]** transformational policies representing fundamental systemic change.

### Key Themes Across Perspectives

1. **Supply-Side Reform:** Multiple agents (Conservative, Progressive, Budget) propose addressing restrictive zoning as root cause
2. **Social Housing Models:** Economic Populist and Progressive propose Vienna/Singapore-style public housing
3. **Tax Incentives:** Budget Expert and Conservative propose expanded tax credits for housing production
4. **Federal Land:** Multiple agents propose transferring federal property for housing development
5. **Security Dimensions:** National Security and International Relations bring unique angles on workforce housing and global talent competition

### Notable Cross-Partisan Agreement

- **Zoning reform** supported by Progressive (with anti-displacement protections), Conservative (deregulation focus), and Budget Expert (regulatory incentive grants)
- **Federal land disposition** proposed by Conservative (market sale), National Security (defense zones), and Budget Expert (land conversion program)
- **Tax incentives** supported across spectrum with different targeting (workforce vs. low-income vs. employer-assisted)
