| PA-06 Conservative | FOR | 10/10 | Core market solution - remove government barriers |
| PA-17 National Security | FOR | 6/10 | Supports workforce housing near bases |

| SA-07 Budget Expert | AGAINST | 4/10 | Conditional funding approach preferred over preemption |

## Analysis: Areas of Agreement

### High Consensus (4+ votes FOR)
1. Zoning reform (with appropriate framing)
2. Tax credit expansion for housing production
3. Federal land disposition for housing

### Moderate Consensus (3 votes FOR)
4. Community Land Trust expansion

### Polarized (2-3 divide)
5. Large-scale social housing
