# Political Viability Assessment: National Zoning Reform

**Policy Council Phase 3 | Political Strategist Analysis**

---

## Overall Assessment: MODERATE VIABILITY

Achievable with right vehicle and coalition management. Unusual bipartisan potential due to economic research consensus and "no new spending" structure.

---

## 1. Congressional Support

### Democratic Caucus: 65-70% Likely Support

**Enthusiastic:** Progressive Caucus, coastal district members, senators from housing-crisis states (CA, NY, MA, WA)

**Hesitant:** Suburban district members, Black Caucus (seeks strong anti-displacement provisions)

### Republican Caucus: 35-45% Potential Support

**Potential supporters:** Libertarian-leaning members, business-district representatives, fiscal hawks

**Likely opponents:** Traditional federalism conservatives, wealthy suburban districts

### Swing Votes
- Moderate Republicans in housing-crisis states
- Suburban Democrats balancing constituent concerns
- Purple-state senators weighing economic benefits

## 3. Strategic Framing

### For Conservatives
**Lead message:** "Removing government barriers to housing construction"

- Local zoning IS government regulation restricting property rights
- Highlight market distortions from exclusionary zoning
- Cite Cato, AEI economists
- Note highway funding precedent (drinking age)

### For Progressives
**Lead message:** "Housing justice with community protections"

- Center racial equity dimensions
- Emphasize environmental justice benefits
- Highlight tenant protections
- Connect to economic mobility agenda

### Bipartisan Bridge
- "Economists across the political spectrum agree"
- "Removes barriers without new federal spending"
- "Workers deserve to afford to live near jobs"

---

## 4. Pathway Analysis

### Legislative Route (Recommended)
**Vehicle:** Infrastructure reauthorization bill

| Aspect | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| Must-pass vehicle | Yes |
| Policy nexus | Clear (housing-transit) |
| Precedent | Funding conditions exist |
| Probability | 40-50% with sustained advocacy |

### Regulatory Route
- Limited without new statutory authority
- Can prioritize in discretionary grant scoring
- Vulnerable to legal challenge and administration change

### Executive Actions
- Executive order signaling priority
- White House Housing Council
- Federal property disposition preferences

---

## 5. Amendments for Coalition Building

### Support-Expanding Amendments
| Amendment | Target | Political Cost |
|-----------|--------|----------------|
| Grandfather existing homeowners | Suburban Rs | Medium |
| Extended phase-in (5-7 years) | Local governments | Low |
| Technical assistance grants | Small cities | Low |
| Prevailing wage requirements | Building trades | Medium |
| Anti-displacement provisions | Civil rights groups | Medium |

### Amendments to Resist
- Full exemptions for any jurisdiction
- Implementation beyond 7 years
- Removal of transit-density linkage
