# National Zoning Reform Act
## One-Page Executive Summary

**Policy Council Phase 3 | Quick Reference**

---

## The Problem

America faces a **3.8 million unit housing shortage**. Research shows restrictive local zoning adds 20-50% to housing costs in high-demand metros, blocking workers from economic opportunity and driving inequality.

---

## The Solution

**Condition federal infrastructure and transit funding on local zoning reforms** that allow housing near transit and jobs.

### Core Requirements for Jurisdictions

| Reform | Standard |
|--------|----------|
| Transit-oriented density | Multifamily by-right within 0.5 miles of transit |
| Accessory dwelling units | Legalized in all residential zones |
| Parking reform | Eliminate minimums near transit |
| Approval timelines | 90-day by-right approvals |

### Anti-Displacement Protections

- Just-cause eviction requirements
- Right of first refusal for existing tenants
- Inclusionary zoning (10-15% affordable units)
- Relocation assistance for displaced residents

## Implementation Timeline

- **Years 1-3:** Pilot program with early adopters (5% bonus)
- **Years 4-6:** Phase-in with technical assistance
- **Year 7+:** Full compliance required

---

## Why This Works

1. **No new federal spending** — restructures existing funding
2. **Clear precedent** — highway funding tied to drinking age, speed limits
3. **Bipartisan economic consensus** — rare left-right agreement
4. **Measurable outcomes** — objective compliance standards
