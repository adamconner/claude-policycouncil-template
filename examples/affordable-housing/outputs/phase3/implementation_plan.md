# Implementation Plan: National Zoning Reform

**Policy Council Phase 3 | Implementation Expert Analysis**

---

## Feasibility Rating: MEDIUM

| Factor | Assessment |
|--------|------------|
| Legal Authority | **Strong** - Spending Clause precedent (highway speed limits, drinking age) |
| Political Viability | **Moderate** - Bipartisan YIMBY movement, but local resistance |
| Administrative Capacity | **Moderate** - HUD/DOT experience with conditional grants |
| Technical Complexity | **High** - Defining compliance across 30,000+ jurisdictions |
| Timeline to Impact | **Long** - Housing construction lags policy by 3-7 years |

---

## Lead Agencies

- **Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)** - Primary policy lead
- **Department of Transportation (DOT)** - Funding conditions implementation
- **Supporting:** Treasury (tax credits), EPA (environmental streamlining)

---

## Phased Implementation Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation (Year 1)

| Quarter | Milestone |
|---------|-----------|
| Q1 | Publish Advance Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (ANPRM) |
| Q2 | Stakeholder listening sessions (50 states, major metros) |
| Q3 | Publish Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM) |
| Q4 | Final rule publication; begin hiring compliance staff |

**Compliance Metrics to Define:**
- Minimum density within 0.5 miles of transit stations
- By-right approval pathways
- Parking maximum standards
- ADU legalization requirements

### Phase 2: Pilot Program (Years 2-3)

| Action | Incentive |
|--------|-----------|
| Launch "Early Adopter" program | 5% bonus on eligible grants |
| Enroll 50-100 pilot jurisdictions | Technical assistance priority |
| First compliance reviews | Maintain bonus for compliant |
| Publish lessons learned | Inform full rollout |

### Phase 3: Full Implementation (Years 4+)

| Year | Requirement | Non-Compliance Consequence |
|------|-------------|---------------------------|
| 4 | States submit compliance plans | Warning; TA offered |
| 5 | Localities demonstrate progress | 5% reduction in formula funds |
| 6 | Full compliance for discretionary grants | Ineligibility for new awards |
| 7+ | Sustained compliance | Up to 15% reduction |

## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|------------|------------|
| Political resistance | High | Extended phase-in, generous TA, state flexibility |
| Legal challenges | Medium | Careful constitutional design, DOJ review |
| Implementation complexity | High | Clear standards, model ordinances, automated tools |
| Displacement concerns | Medium | Integrate anti-displacement requirements |

## Success Factors

1. Clear, objective, measurable compliance standards
2. Adequate lead time (3-5 years for code reform)
3. Robust technical assistance for capacity-limited jurisdictions

5. Data-driven adaptation based on outcomes
