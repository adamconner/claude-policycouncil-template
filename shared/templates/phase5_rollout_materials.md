## Deliverable 2: Op-Ed Draft (800-1000 words)

```markdown
# [COMPELLING HEADLINE]
## [Subhead that provides context]

*By [Author Name], [Title/Credentials]*

[HOOK - Opening paragraph: Start with a compelling story, surprising statistic, or provocative question that draws readers in. 2-3 sentences.]

[PROBLEM - Second paragraph: Define the problem clearly. Who is affected? What are the stakes? Use specific examples.]

[CONTEXT - Third paragraph: Briefly explain how we got here. What's the current state of affairs? What has failed?]

[SOLUTION - Fourth paragraph: Introduce your policy solution. What is it? How does it work? Be specific but accessible.]

[MECHANISM - Fifth paragraph: Explain key implementation details. How would this actually happen? Address practical concerns.]

[EVIDENCE - Sixth paragraph: Cite evidence supporting your approach. Academic research, precedents, pilot programs, expert opinions.]

[ADDRESSING OBJECTIONS - Seventh paragraph: Acknowledge and rebut the strongest counterarguments. Be fair but firm.]

[BROADER VISION - Eighth paragraph: Connect to larger values and goals. Why does this matter beyond the immediate issue?]

[CALL TO ACTION - Final paragraph: What should readers do? What happens next? End with forward momentum.]

---

*[Author bio: 1-2 sentences including relevant credentials and contact/organization information]*
```

**Text:** "Congress finds that—
(1) [Finding 1];
(2) [Finding 2];
(3) [Finding 3]."

---

**Text:** "In this Act—
(1) the term '[term]' means [definition];
(2) the term '[term]' means [definition]."

**Analysis:** Critical section defining scope. [Explanation of key definitional choices.]

---

#### Section 4: [Core Mechanism]

#### Section 5: [Secondary Mechanism]

**Analysis:** [Detailed explanation]

#### Section 6: Enforcement
**Text:** "[Enforcement provisions]"

**Analysis:** [Explanation of enforcement mechanisms, penalties, and responsible agencies.]

#### Section 7: Appropriations
**Text:** "There is authorized to be appropriated $[X] for each of fiscal years [Year] through [Year]."

**Analysis:** [Budget justification and allocation breakdown.]

#### Section 8: Effective Date
