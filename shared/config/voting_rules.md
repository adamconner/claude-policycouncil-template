## Voting Types

### Type 1: Effectiveness Vote

**Purpose:** Identify policies most likely to achieve the core goals, regardless of ideological preference.

**Weighting Scheme:** EQUAL WEIGHT (1.0 for all 24 voting agents)

| Agent Category | IDs | Weight Each |
|----------------|-----|-------------|
| Political Spectrum | PA-01 to PA-07 | 1.0 |
| Domain-Specific | PA-08 to PA-12 | 1.0 |
| Stakeholder Groups | PA-13 to PA-16 | 1.0 |
| Specialists | SA-01 to SA-08 | 1.0 |

**Total Points Available:** 24.0 per policy

**Instruction to Agents:** Vote based on what you believe will be MOST EFFECTIVE at achieving the policy goals, NOT based on what your ideological perspective would prefer.

---

### Type 2: Compromise Vote

**Purpose:** Identify policies that could achieve broad cross-ideological support.

**Weighting Scheme:** EQUAL WEIGHT (1.0 for all 24 voting agents)

**Total Points Available:** 24.0 per policy

**Instruction to Agents:** Vote for policies you could genuinely support or accept as a reasonable compromise, even if they aren't your first choice.

**Focus:** Identify LARGER, more substantial policy ideas that could bridge ideological divides.

## Voting Procedures

### Step 1: Policy Presentation
- Council Director presents each policy with summary
- Original proposing analyst may provide brief context
- Clarifying questions permitted

### Step 2: Deliberation
- Open discussion among agents
- Arguments for and against documented
- Specialist agents provide technical input

### Step 3: Vote Casting
Each agent provides:
1. **Vote:** YES / NO / ABSTAIN
2. **Reasoning:** 1-2 sentence explanation
3. **Confidence:** HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW

### Step 4: Tally and Recording
- Council Director calculates weighted totals
- Results documented in standard format
- Dissents recorded prominently

## Ranking Procedures

### Top Policies by Effectiveness
1. All policies receive Effectiveness Vote
2. Rank by weighted score (highest to lowest)
3. Select top N (typically 20-40)
4. Ensure minimum coverage per goal

### Top Compromise Policies
1. All policies receive Compromise Vote
2. Filter to policies scoring > 50% support (12.0+ points)
3. Rank by weighted score
4. Focus on substantial policies

### Ideological Rankings
1. Apply appropriate weighted scheme
2. Rank by weighted score
3. Select top 25 for each ideology

## Quorum Requirements

- Minimum 20 of 24 voting agents must participate
- Council Director may proceed with fewer if absence justified
- Missing votes recorded as ABSENT (not abstain)

---

## Conflict of Interest Rules

### Disclosure Requirements
- Industry agents disclose when voting on industry-benefiting policies
- Finance agents disclose when voting on market-affecting policies
- All agents must disclose potential conflicts

### Recusal
- Agents NOT required to recuse for ideological alignment
- Agents MAY be asked to recuse for direct conflicts
- Council Director decides recusal questions

---

## Dissent Documentation

- Record minority opinions when agents disagree
- Include: Agent ID, position, reasoning, suggested alternative
- Add "Dissenting Views" section to master documents
- No suppression of substantive disagreements
